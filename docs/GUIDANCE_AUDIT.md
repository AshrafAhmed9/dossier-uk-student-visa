# Guidance audit — 24 August 2026

This is a small, reproducible check of public university guidance against the
committed rule graph. It is not a claim about the overall quality of any
institution's advice.

## Method

Six university-maintained pages or PDFs returned by searches for UK Student
visa maintenance evidence were checked manually against Appendix Student and
Appendix Finance. The check covered only the amounts, 28-day holding period,
31-day evidence recency, and the in-country 12-month exemption.

## Result

- Four pages reflected the current £1,529 London / £1,171 outside-London
  monthly amounts, or their nine-month equivalents.
- Two older PDFs displayed previous maintenance figures (£1,334 / £1,023).
- The current pages checked also described the 28-day and 31-day timing rules.

The two stale pages are deliberately not identified here. The project does not
name an institution publicly unless Ashraf has first approved a factual notice
and allowed a reasonable opportunity to correct it.

## Primary sources used by the product

- [Appendix Student](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-student)
- [Appendix Finance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-finance)

The committed graph remains `REQUIRES_HUMAN_REVIEW`; this audit does not
replace line-by-line legal review of that graph.
